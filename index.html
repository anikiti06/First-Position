Investing basics in five minutes

Six ideas every guide on this page builds on. Words with a dotted underline show a definition when you hover or tap them.

3–6 monthsof expenses, saved first

Build an emergency fund before investing

Keep this cash in an FDIC-insured high-yield savings account, which pays up to 4.70% right now. Investments can drop right when you need the money.

4% vs 10%T-bill today vs stocks' long-run average

Risk and return travel together

Safer investments pay less. A T-bill pays about 4% with almost no risk. Stocks have averaged around 10% a year over long periods but can fall 30–50% in a crash.

$26,000from $150 a month for 10 years at 7%

Compounding does the heavy lifting

You'd put in $18,000; growth adds about $8,000. Your returns start earning returns of their own, so starting early matters more than starting big.

500companies in one S&P 500 fund

Don't put all your eggs in one basket

An index fund holds hundreds of companies, so one company's bad news barely moves it. A single stock can fall 50% on its own.

−26%of 25-year gains lost to a 1% yearly fee

Fees and account type matter

Growing at 6% instead of 7% for 25 years costs about a quarter of your gains. A Roth IRA lets investments grow tax-free if you have income from a job.

3 trade-offsreturn, risk, liquidity

Every investment balances three things

How much it can earn, how much it can drop, and how fast you can get your money out. The risk dots and liquidity labels on this page show where each one sits.

Methodology & sources

Risk score

0–100, built from loss tolerance (45%), time horizon (35%) and primary goal (20%). A horizon under one year caps the score at 15, because money you need soon shouldn't ride the market.

Fit score

  • Starts at 100.
  • −22 per risk level above your tolerance; −6 per level below.
  • −30 if the typical holding period is longer than your horizon.
  • −20 to −30 if it locks money up when you need access.
  • −35 if the realistic minimum exceeds your starting amount.
  • −10 per level of effort above your preference; +8 per interest match.
  • New investors: −12 for complex investments (individual stocks and bonds, rental property, raw land, crowdfunding, crypto) and +5 for simple, low-risk ones.

Projection

Monthly compounding of the model allocation. Expected return is the weighted mix; volatility is the weighted mix scaled by 0.85 for diversification. The 10th/90th percentile band uses ±1.28 standard deviations of the annualized return over your horizon.
