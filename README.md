# First Position

An AI-assisted investing guide for people starting out. A short profile quiz matches users to 18 beginner-accessible investments (high-yield savings, Treasuries, bonds, index funds, REITs, rental property, raw land, crypto and more), with layouts for new, intermediate and expert investors.

**Live site:** https://anikiti06.github.io/First-Position/

## Features
- Investor profile quiz with a 0–100 risk score and ranked fit scores
- New / Intermediate / Expert layouts (plain-English glossary for beginners; allocation builder, risk/return matrix and position sizer for experts)
- Investment library with how-to-start guides, risks and tax notes
- Starter allocation and interactive growth projection
- Pro research agent: briefings on specific holdings (Apple, IREN, Bitcoin, Iowa farmland, Palatine IL housing, Treasury rates) with price history, news, projections and market signals
- Market snapshot sourced from the Federal Reserve H.15, BLS CPI and other public data (Nov 2026)

Educational project; not investment advice.
